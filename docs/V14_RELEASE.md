# v0.14 — Camp Supply Market

8 November 2026 (Malaysia).

Choose **Next: Stock the camp** when the ferry docks, or open **Camp Supply Market** under Field missions on the map. The four short missions are now grouped there; the chapter routes remain available below them.

With RM100, buy at least two food packs (RM12 each), one first-aid kit (RM20) and one lantern (RM15). Keep at least RM20 for tomorrow, so the shopping total may not exceed RM80. Item subtotals, remaining cash and the reserve marker respond to every change. Feedback distinguishes missing essentials, overspending and spending that fits RM100 but leaves too little in reserve. Four different baskets satisfy all constraints.

Check a valid basket, unpack the supplies and light the lanterns. The scene shows the actual number of items purchased. The **Camp Quartermaster** badge persists in the current browser. Replaying the market does not change lesson evidence, expedition XP, materials or equipment; teacher links open their assigned route directly.

For a classroom pilot, ask students to find two baskets that work and explain why a basket costing RM83 fails even though it costs less than RM100. The game supports English and Bahasa Melayu; simulated touch checks do not replace a pilot on real devices.
