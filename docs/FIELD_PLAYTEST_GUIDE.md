# Field mission playtest · v0.17

Invite 6–10 students to play on their usual phones for around 15 minutes. Share the [student playtest link](https://sunshine-quest.pages.dev/?view=regions&playtest=1). Let students try the route independently and keep partial reports when the time is up.

## Students

1. Enter a nickname and choose **Start recording and play** to take part. **Back to map without recording** opens the regular game map.
2. Follow the next mission from each victory screen. A refresh of the same tab returns to the playtest panel. **Continue the playtest** starts the first unfinished mission from its beginning.
3. At the end of Storm Supply Relay, choose **Playtest report**. To send a partial report, go back to the region map and choose **Playtest report** there.
4. Choose **Stop recording**, then **Download my CSV**. Share the file as instructed by your teacher before closing the tab.

## Teachers

1. Open the regular region map and choose **Field playtest → Import student CSVs**. Import up to 30 files at a time. A newer export from the same tab replaces an earlier export from that session.
2. Compare how many students started and completed each mission. Look at incorrect checks, hint opens and the median time to the first check. Use these alongside observations about controls and instructions.
3. Choose **Download summary CSV** before refreshing or closing the teacher page. The teacher summary is held in memory.

Student reports use a random session ID. They contain mission visits, completion and check activity, with no nickname or learner code. The report stays in the current tab and the game does not upload it. Existing field badges do not count as completed in the report. Active seconds accumulate across visits while the mission is open and visible, including retries. They include time spent on instructions and movement.

| Observe on the phones | What to write down |
|---|---|
| Walk to the bridge and choose anchors | Did students discover the hold-to-move control? Where did they first ask for help? |
| Check a plan and recover from an error | Which message, label or input caused hesitation? Could students continue after reading it? |
| Finish or leave a mission early | Could students find their report and download it? |
| Compare the ridge and valley in the relay | How did students explain the difference in money left over? |
