# v0.12 — Supply Cart Run

8 November 2026 (Malaysia).

The bridge victory screen now offers **Next: Drive the supply cart**, alongside the region map and replay. Returning guest players can also select **Supply Cart Run** on the map. Teacher-assigned missions retain their original routes.

The student selects an initial speed and duration. The cart then travels at 2 m/s for the remainder of an 8 s trip. The shaded areas on the speed–time graph, total distance and predicted stop all follow the selected plan. The depot is 28 m away. A short or long route reports the difference in metres and keeps boarding locked. There are four exact solutions in the controls: 8 m/s for 2 s, 6 m/s for 3 s, 5 m/s for 4 s or 4 m/s for 6 s during stage one.

After checking a working plan, board and depart. The same model drives the rider, cart and graph cursor. Students can choose **Show arrival** if they prefer to skip the ride animation. At the depot, unload the crates to earn the local **Supply Runner** badge. Keyboard and touch controls, English and BM, and reduced-motion behavior are supported. This is an optional side adventure; rewards and retries do not change classroom evidence, expedition XP or equipment.

For a pilot, observe whether students choose the new next-mission button after finishing the bridge, whether they distinguish a short trip from an overshoot, and whether they can explain what each shaded rectangle represents. Test the controls on real student phones before drawing conclusions about ease of use.
