# v0.13 — Crystal Signal

8 November 2026 (Malaysia).

Choose **Next: Signal the ferry** after unloading the cart, or choose **Crystal Signal** on the region map. The map and replay options remain available after either mission. Teacher assignments still use their original lesson routes.

The student chooses how to divide six crystals between red and blue and whether to return the first crystal before the second draw. The probability tree recomputes both conditional branches and the sum for different colours. Either 2 red and 4 blue or 4 red and 2 blue reaches the target of 8/15 when drawing without replacement. Returning the first crystal leaves the denominator at six and cannot reach the target.

After approving the bag, trace red then blue and blue then red. The bag visibly loses its first crystal, and every new demonstration starts with all six. A same-colour or repeated order does not light a second lantern. Once both lamps are lit, send the signal and watch the ferry dock to earn the local **Signal Keeper** badge. The player chooses the paths; no random draw decides completion. **Show docking** skips the animation if desired.

The badge is saved in the current browser. It does not change the original route, equipment, XP, or classroom evidence. The interface supports English and Bahasa Melayu with touch and keyboard controls. In the classroom pilot, ask students why the second denominator falls from six to five, and why the two orders are added rather than multiplied together.
