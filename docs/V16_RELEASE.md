# v0.16 — Storm Supply Relay

8 November 2026 (Malaysia).

After earning all five Field mission badges, open **Storm Supply Relay** from the map. If Canyon Landing completes the set, its victory screen also offers a direct next step. Earlier badges count automatically. The final relay has a separate local badge and does not change expedition progress or the existing teacher finale.

The student must reach the shelter in 8 s and keep at least RM20 from a budget of RM90. Supplies cost RM36. The 28 m ridge route has a RM10 permit; the 32 m valley route has a RM4 permit. Choose a speed and duration for stage one; stage two is 2 m/s for the remaining time. The cart fee is RM(3 × first speed + 2 × first time).

Distance and reserve are checked independently. For example, 8 m/s for 2 s reaches 28 m on the ridge but leaves only RM16. A ridge plan of 5 m/s for 4 s leaves RM21. A valley plan of 6 m/s for 4 s leaves RM24. There are four accepted plans, and an approved plan must be checked again after editing.

Send the cart, watch it follow the selected route, and unload at the shelter. The receipt shows distance, spending and the money remaining. **Show arrival** skips the animation if desired. English and Bahasa Melayu are supported. For a classroom pilot, ask students why the longer route can leave more money and why the exact-distance ridge plan with RM16 is rejected.
