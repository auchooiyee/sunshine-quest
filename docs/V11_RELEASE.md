# v0.11 — Broken Bridge Rescue

8 November 2026 (Malaysia).

This short, bilingual Quadratic Forest mission links a graph to an action the student performs: walking over the bridge they designed. First-time guest players see it after choosing a nickname. Returning players can start or replay it from the region map or the expedition intro. The **Leave mission** button returns to the previous entry; classroom links still open the assigned mission directly.

1. Walk to Aina’s survey post using A/D, arrow keys or the touch buttons.
2. Select the two x-intercepts on the arch trace or use the numbered buttons. Incorrect choices are marked on the model, and a hint is available on request.
3. Adjust span and curve factor. The preview and peak height respond immediately. The endpoints must reach both banks, and the peak must match the 9 m guide.
4. Cross the finished arch and light the beacon. The Bridge Builder badge is remembered locally. Completing or replaying the starter does not change lesson evidence, XP, materials, equipment or classroom assignments.

The starter reuses the game’s forest and explorer art with a dedicated river, bridge, survey overlay and beacon drawn in SVG. The bridge preview and walking surface both use the game’s quadratic model. Touch targets and reduced-motion behavior are included. The starter is a separate small route, not a replacement of the full Quadratic Forest chapter.

For a classroom pilot, give 6–10 students the public guest link and observe whether they reach the survey post unaided, finish the bridge on their own phone and voluntarily choose another route or replay. Ask them to explain what their mathematical choices changed in the forest. The 60–90 second first-success target should be measured with students; browser checks verify behavior, not classroom timing or learning outcomes.
